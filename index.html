Welcome to La Finca

Set on five acres of olive groves and wild herbs, La Finca is a lovingly restored 19th-century farmhouse that blends rustic character with modern comfort.

Spend your days by the private pool, explore nearby white villages, or simply relax on the shaded terrace with a glass of local wine as the sun sets over the mountains.
